An ordered change in [state](/concepts/databases/State.md) that propagates forward in [time](/processes/time/Time.md) until its termination

A process is how things happen. It encompasses state transitions, indirection, and the intentions behind them.

Processes are listed in /processes directory in a project.

Examples of reflections of a process:

- Process diagrams
- Code with the process algorithms
- A description of the process

### Examples: 

- a marketing project could have a process for a customer's journey
- a climate model has the water cycle process
- any activity of a subject within ontology can and should be represented as a process
- a thought process of how a naming decision should be made
