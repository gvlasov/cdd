Substances are concepts that denote some kind of quantifiable matter. Substances are not used in plural in an ontology unlike the common concepts, but can still have reflections, descriptions

### Examples

Directly relates to CDD:

- "Source code" is a substance, quantifies in characters/lines
- "Attention span" is a substance, quantified in seconds or a number of simultaneously held attention targets
- Time is a substance, quantified in seconds
- Reality is a substance, quantified in the number of concepts and their reflections that take part in the reality, or in time spent running that reality

General examples from life:

- Water is a substance, quantified in liters
- Tolerance is a substance, quantified in how much more annoyance one can take before losing it
- Annoyance is a substance, quantified in what total magnitude of problems has to be solved before annoyance disappears
