Substances are concepts that denote some kind of quantifiable matter. Substances are not used in plural in an ontology unlike the common concepts, but can still have reflections, descriptions

Substances are often attributes

### Examples

Directly relates to CDD:

- "Source code" is a substance, quantifies in characters/lines, attribute of a project
- "Attention span" is a substance, quantified in seconds or a number of simultaneously held attention targets, attribute of a developer
- Time is a substance, quantified in seconds, attribute of a reality
- Reality is a substance, quantified in the number of concepts and their reflections that take part in the reality, or in time spent running that reality, attribute of a subject
- "Help" as a noun, quantified in characters or items or the amount of functionality covered, attribute of a project command shell

General examples from life:

- Water is a substance, quantified in liters
- Tolerance is a substance, quantified in how much more annoyance one can take before losing it
- Annoyance is a substance, quantified in what total magnitude of problems has to be solved before annoyance disappears
